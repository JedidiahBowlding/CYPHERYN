# Code of Conduct

SignalTrace contributors must help maintain a professional, inclusive, and security-conscious community.

Be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive review, protect private information, and focus disagreement on technical evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of credentials or private security reports, and unsafe testing are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens people, systems, or the community. Report conduct concerns through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting mechanism when confidentiality is necessary; otherwise contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and protect reporter privacy where practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing SignalTrace publicly.
